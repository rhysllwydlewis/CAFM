What happened
Member of staff slipped on a wet floor near the back-of-house sink while carrying stock. Bruising to left elbow; first aid given on site. Floor had been mopped 10 minutes earlier; no wet-floor sign displayed.
Root cause analysis (5 whys)
Why 1Why did she slip? Floor was wet.
Why 2Why was the floor wet? Mopped during trading hours.
Why 3Why no sign? Signs stored in another room.
Why 4Why stored elsewhere? Cleaning cupboard reorganised in October.
RootRoot cause: cleaning SOP not updated after layout change.
